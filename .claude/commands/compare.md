Compare **$ARGUMENTS** using the wiki.

1. **Find the pages:** by slug, by name, or by filtering a section's frontmatter for what the
   question asks (for example, robot arms with a cost under 300 USD).
2. **Read the frontmatter first.** Open the page bodies only for what the frontmatter does not
   hold.
3. **Build one table:** a row per page; columns for what the question asks, plus cost with its
   date, `evidence` and `last_verified`.
4. **Never fill a gap.** Show `—` for unknown values and say which ones are missing. Point out costs
   older than a year and values that are only `vendor-claim`.
5. **Answer** in two to four sentences, with the condition the answer depends on. If the same
   comparison is likely to come up again, offer to save it as a decision guide in `wiki/guides/`.
